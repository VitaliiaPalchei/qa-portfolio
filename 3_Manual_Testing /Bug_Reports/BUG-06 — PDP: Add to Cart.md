
## Title: Out-of-stock product can be added to the cart

**Environment:** Chrome, macOS

**Preconditions:**
Product status is Out of Stock.

**Steps to Reproduce:**
1. Open an out-of-stock product PDP.
2. Click Add to Cart.
   
**Actual Result:**
An out-of-stock product is added to the cart.

**Expected Result:**
The 'Add to Cart' button should be disabled or hidden for out-of-stock products.

**Severity:** High

**Priority:** High
