
## Title: User can proceed to checkout without accepting Terms of Service

**Environment:** Chrome, macOS

**Preconditions:**
At least one product is in the cart.

**Steps to Reproduce:**
1. Open the Cart page.
2. Do not select the “I agree with the terms of service” checkbox.
3. Click Proceed to Checkout.

**Expected Result:**
The user should be blocked and shown a validation message. 

**Actual Result:**
User is redirected to checkout.

**Severity:**  High
**Priority:**  High
